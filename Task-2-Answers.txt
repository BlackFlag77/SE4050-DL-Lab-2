Question 1: What happens when the number of hidden nodes increase?

As the number of hidden nodes increases, the neural network gains a greater ability to learn complex patterns in the dataset. 
The accuracy improved significantly when the hidden layer size increased from 1-2 nodes to 3-5 nodes. 
However, further increasing the number of hidden nodes beyond 5 did not improve performance and slightly reduced accuracy.

Question 2: Can you explain the pattern of the accuracy when the hidden nodes increase?

When the network contains only 1 or 2 hidden nodes, it does not have enough capacity to model the complex flower-shaped decision boundary, resulting in underfitting and low accuracy. 
Increasing the hidden nodes to 3-5 gives the network sufficient learning capacity, causing a large increase in accuracy. 
However, using 20 or 50 hidden nodes does not further improve performance because the problem is relatively simple and additional neurons do not provide useful information. 
The slight decrease in accuracy may be caused by overfitting or optimization difficulties.
